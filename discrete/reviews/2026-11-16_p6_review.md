### 4. Tile rows do not always stay on one line
- **Severity:** MINOR (layout). Not fixed.
- **What happens:** `w-word` with 12 letters (the allowed maximum) breaks into two lines at 390 px, and with 11 or 12
  letters at 360 px. `w-stars` with n = 8, k = 4 (11 cells) breaks into two lines at 360 px. All other sizes stay on
  one line, the page is never wider than the screen, nothing overlaps.
- **Why the builder's check passes:** `p6_widgets_check.mjs` asserts `w * cells + gaps <= 264 || w === 22`. At the
  minimum tile width of 22 px the row is 282 px wide for 11 cells, and the second half of the condition lets it pass.
- **For the main session:** either accept the wrap, or lower the letter limit (question R3).

### 5. `w-word`: letters outside modern spelling
- **Severity:** MINOR. Not fixed.
- «և» is one letter, «ու» / «Ու» / «ՈՒ» is one letter, upper and lower case are the same letter, Latin and Armenian
  letters are different letters («aա» has 2! = 2 arrangements). All of this matches the rule the widget states.
- «եւ» (classical spelling) is two letters ե, ւ; «ԵՎ» in capitals becomes «եվ», two letters. Consistent, but a
  reader who types «ԵՎ» for «և» gets 2.
- A word with a stand-alone «ո» and a stand-alone «ւ» next to an «ու» is counted by units: the units ո, ւ, ու have
  6 arrangements but only 5 different strings. Not reachable with a modern Armenian word.
- An emoji is two halves for the script; the message prints «Հեռացրեք՝ «�», «�»։».
