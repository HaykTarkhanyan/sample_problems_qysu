## Findings so far

### 1. The two non-identities are written with a bare «≠»
- **Severity:** UNCLEAR. **Not fixed yet** (fix planned, see "What remains").
- **What the page says** (`#exam`, box «Նույնության նման, բայց ոչ նույնություն»):
  «$A\cup(B\symd C)\neq(A\cup B)\symd(A\cup C)$ ...» and «$(A\cup B)\times(A\cup B)\neq(A\times A)\cup(B\times B)$ ...»,
  each with one counterexample.
- **What is right:** neither is an identity, and both counterexamples are correct, but «≠» is false for many sets.
  The second is an equality exactly when $A\subseteq B$ or $B\subseteq A$ (454 of the 1024 pairs over a 5-set, for
  example $A=B$ or $A=\varnothing$). Proof: if $A\subseteq B$ both sides are $B\times B$; if neither inclusion holds,
  take $a\in A\setminus B$, $b\in B\setminus A$, then $(a,b)$ is on the left only. The first is an equality exactly
  when $A=\varnothing$ (the right side is $(B\symd C)\setminus A$, the left is $A\cup(B\symd C)$). The heading and
  the word «Հակաօրինակ» point the right way, and the `w-prod` widget does say «Այս ընտրությամբ կողմերը համընկնում են,
  բայց սա նույնություն չէ», but a student can take the formula away as "never equal".
- The predict block of problem 60 is fine: it states «Չհատվող ոչ դատարկ $A$, $B$-ի դեպքում» (without "non-empty"
  it would be false: $A=\varnothing$, $B=\{1\}$ are disjoint and the sides are equal).
