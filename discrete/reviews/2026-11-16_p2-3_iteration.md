## 0. Mathematical error found in the existing content (fixed on the page and in the sheet)

Problem 3.3, the check paragraph said the computer enumeration over $-20\le x\le 20$ found the two compositions
unequal, «առաջին անհամապատասխանությունն արդեն $x=1$-ում» (the first mismatch already at $x=1$). That is false:
$(x+2)^2=x^2+2 \iff 4x+4=2 \iff x=-\tfrac12$, so $\rho\cdot\sigma$ and $\sigma\cdot\rho$ differ at **every** integer.
In the enumeration order the first mismatch is at $x=-20$, and already $x=0$ gives $4\ne2$. New wording (page and
sheet): «... և գտավ դրանք անհավասար ամեն $x$-ի համար ($(x+2)^2=x^2+2$ հավասարությունից կհետևեր $x=-\tfrac12$, որն
ամբողջ թիվ չէ)։» Asserted in `p2-3_check.mjs`. The answer of 3.3 was right; only this sentence about the check was not.

No other mathematical error found. A loose wording is put to the owner below (Q5).
