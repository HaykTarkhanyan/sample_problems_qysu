- **Severity:** WRONG. **Fixed.** Introduced by this iteration (the old widget ended with the inclusion-exclusion
  line; the builder added both the predict block and a new last line).
- **What the page said:** the why-text of the question "how many of 1..699 are divisible by 3 or 5" ends with
